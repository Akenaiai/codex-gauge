# 0.4.7: quieter creative credit expiry reminders

## English

Creative credit batches of **100 credits or less** now keep ordinary balance/date text without expiry emphasis, priority sorting or expiry notifications. Each creative platform card has **Reminder settings** for its own inclusive cutoff; 0 allows any positive balance. The rule checks the expiring batch rather than the account total. Daily gifts stay silent, unknown expiring amounts stay ordinary, and percentage/request units are not treated as credits.

Eligible batches expiring within seven days move to the top, ordered by the nearest deadline. Amber labels provide advance notice, silent notifications begin within three days, and the final-day label turns red with one final silent notice. Date-only records preserve day precision and request verification. **Dismiss this batch** clears that account/pool/expiry's emphasis and future notices across restarts while retaining balances, dates and other batches.

The Codex gauge keeps its quota reading and reset marker. A separate creative image icon and flip-entry count identify creative expiry; notification titles, groups and click destinations distinguish creative credits from Codex. Creative expiry opens the back; ordinary reopening still starts on Codex. Other tool expiry notices open the full credit center.

The Windows release retains the verified 0.4.7-preview.1 functionality. Verification includes 277 automated checks, 14 isolated native UI checks of the same feature code (Chinese/English, dark/light, narrow viewport, settings persistence, dismissal and both reminder markers), and packaged first-run/legacy-profile checks. Release artifacts are built from a fixed source snapshot and checked against their contents. Public screenshots contain synthetic data; the English example uses a custom Suno cutoff of 5 credits, while the default is 100.

Existing settings, accounts, date rules and connections are retained. No additional provider login, generation request, telemetry or service is introduced. Windows binaries remain unsigned. Natural future notification delivery, real Windows notification-center activation and Mac behavior remain field-validation limits.

## 简体中文

创作积分**100分及以下**保留普通余额和日期，不加临期强调、不因到期置顶，也不发送到期通知。每个平台卡片可在**提醒设置**中单独修改包含边界的忽略线，填0表示有正积分即可提醒。判断的是即将失效的那批积分，而非账户总额。每日赠送继续静音，临期金额未知时仅显示普通日期；百分比、次数不按积分处理。

符合条件且7天内到期的积分自动置顶，按到期时间从近到远排列。提前用琥珀色标签提示，3天内开始静默通知，最后一天标签标红并最后静默提醒一次。只有日期的记录按天显示、提示核实，不编造时刻。**本批不再提醒**针对账号/积分池/到期日取消强调和后续通知，重启后仍保留，余额、日期和其他批次不受影响。

Codex额度读数及原重置标记保留，另用创作图片图标和翻面计数标识创作临期；通知标题、分组和点击目标区分创作积分与Codex。创作到期进入背面，普通关闭后重开仍默认Codex。其他工具到期通知进入完整额度中心。

Windows正式版保留已验证0.4.7-preview.1的功能。验证包括277项自动检查、同一功能代码的14项隔离原生界面检查（中英、明暗、窄视口、设置保存、单批静音、双标记）及包内首次/旧配置检查。发布产物从固定源码快照构建并核对内容。公开截图均为合成示例；英文图将Suno忽略线自定义为5分，默认仍为100分。

原设置、账号、日期规则和连接保留。没有新增平台登录、生成请求、分析追踪或服务。Windows文件仍未代码签名。自然未来通知送达、真实Windows通知中心点击和Mac行为保留待现场验证范围。
