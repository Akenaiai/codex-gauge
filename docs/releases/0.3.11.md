# 0.3.11 — Keep the creative back focused on balances

- Shows only creative tools with current balance readings. Available credits and confirmed zero balances both remain visible.
- Moves unconnected, failed and unconfirmed readings out of the compact back; they remain available in the credit center. Removes the unconfirmed counter from the back.
- Hidden tools continue refreshing and reappear after a successful reading. Preserves expiry priority, countdowns, daily-gift suppression, and the original Codex face.

Windows builds remain unsigned; Mac behavior has not been device-tested.
