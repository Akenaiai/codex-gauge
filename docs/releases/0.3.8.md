# 0.3.8: Quiet daily-credit expiries

Daily gifts that clear the same day no longer trigger expiry notifications, red expiry styling, priority placement or compact expiry countdowns. Their balances and factual dates remain in Details, Calendar and read-only MCP output. Daily expiry facts carry `notify: false`.

This is based on explicit daily type/cadence or recognized daily-gift metadata, never simply an expiry date of today. Subscription and purchased credits expiring today keep their normal reminders. Explicit non-daily types and stated multi-day validity take precedence over legacy daily names. Unclassified dates bound to a verified pool inherit its cadence; the date editor reflects this and explains why daily expiry reminders are off.

Explicitly configured renewal reminders are preserved. No balances, dates, reminder history, account connections or original Codex preferences are deleted. Windows remains unsigned; Mac and natural future notification delivery retain their existing field-test limits.

## 中文

每日赠送、当天清零的积分，不再触发到期通知、标红、置顶或背面的到期倒计时；余额和日期事实仍保留在详情、日历及只读MCP中，每日到期事件标为 `notify: false`。

按明确的每日类型或每日赠送信息识别，不会仅因为“今天到期”就静音。月度、购买积分恰好今天到期，仍正常提醒；明确的非每日类型和多日有效期优先于旧名称。未分类的绑定日期会继承已核实积分池的类型，日期表单同步说明每日到期不提醒。

单独配置的恢复提醒保留，不删除余额、日期、提醒历史、已有连接或原Codex偏好。安装包仍未签名，Mac与自然未来通知保留待验范围。
