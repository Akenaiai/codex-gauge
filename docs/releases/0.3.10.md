# 0.3.10 — Compact panel fit and reopening behavior

- Keeps creative credit cards inside the compact panel. Long account/date notes wrap within the card, while the list continues to scroll vertically.
- Reopening a closed panel always starts with Codex usage. Flipping remains available during the current opening, without writing a persistent face preference. Older saved face selections are ignored.
- Preserves credit readings, date provenance, expiry priority and daily-gift notification rules.

Validated with automated checks and isolated native Electron interactions, including long date/account notes at actual 356px and 320px window widths in Chinese/dark and English/light, plus repeated close/reopen and legacy-preference cases. Windows builds remain unsigned; Mac behavior has not been device-tested.
